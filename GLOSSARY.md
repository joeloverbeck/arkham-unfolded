# Arkham Unfolded

Terms for this application's play, presentation and external source material.

## Language

**Active investigator**:
The investigator whose turn is currently in progress. Other investigators may still participate through effects and opportunities permitted by the rules.
_Avoid_: Selected investigator, focused investigator

**Testing investigator**:
The investigator performing the current skill test, including a test outside their own turn. Other investigators may contribute without becoming the testing investigator.
_Avoid_: Active investigator when referring only to who performs a test

**Camera focus**:
The tabletop area currently framed for viewing. It does not determine whose turn it is or who performs a game choice.
_Avoid_: Active investigator, investigator activation

**Action marker**:
A tabletop reminder representing one action allowance and whether it has been spent. It is a presentation component, not an additional rules-defined token or a guarantee that a particular action is currently legal.
_Avoid_: Chaos token, action button

**Restricted additional action**:
An extra action whose use is limited by the effect that grants it, distinct from the investigator's ordinary action allowance.
_Avoid_: Ordinary action when referring to a restricted allowance

**Scenario area**:
The shared playing surface for the location map, investigator standees and scenario components, including the act, agenda, encounter deck, encounter discard pile and victory display.
_Avoid_: Player area, map area when referring to all shared components

**Map area**:
The part of the scenario area containing locations, their connections and the investigator standees at those locations. It is a tabletop arrangement, not an additional rules-defined game zone.
_Avoid_: Scenario area when referring only to the map

**Enemy sideboard**:
The tabletop display of enemy cards that remain at locations rather than in an investigator's threat area, grouped by their location. It is a presentation arrangement, not a rules-defined game zone.
_Avoid_: Threat area, enemy deck, off-map zone

**Enemy-presence marker**:
A tabletop marker linking a location to its group on the enemy sideboard. Its count represents that group's cards, not every enemy sharing the location with investigators.
_Avoid_: Enemy token, total enemies at the location

**Investigator area**:
An individual investigator's personal playing surface, distinct from the shared scenario area. One human player can control several investigators, each with their own area.
_Avoid_: Player table when referring to an investigator's area

**Asset slot**:
A unit of an investigator's capacity for controlled assets, whose type and occupancy are specified by slot symbols and applicable card effects. An asset can occupy multiple slots or none; its traits alone do not determine slot occupancy.
_Avoid_: Location standee slot, asset card count

**Effective slot capacity**:
The investigator's current slot allowance after applicable effects, including any restrictions on which assets can use additional slots.
_Avoid_: Printed capacity when referring to the modified allowance

**Keys**:
A card-defined type of use, such as the two uses on Old Keyring. Keys are distinct from other use types such as ammo, charges and supplies.
_Avoid_: Charges when referring to keys

**Test area**:
The shared presentation space dedicated to the current skill test, its source, commitments and chaos-token resolution, with the chaos bag and scenario reference kept there between tests. It is a tabletop arrangement, not a rules-defined game zone or a general area for resolving card effects.
_Avoid_: Threat area, investigator area, resolution tray

**Resolution tray**:
An investigator-associated temporary presentation space for searches, revelations and other card or token effects outside skill-test resolution. It is a tabletop arrangement, not a rules-defined game zone; components retain their rules-defined zones until an effect moves them.
_Avoid_: Test area, discard pile

**Encounter discard pile**:
The shared, ordered, face-up pile of discarded cards owned by the encounter deck. Its contents are public information and out of play; card abilities and rules can specify a different destination.
_Avoid_: Victory display, investigator discard pile

**Victory display**:
The shared out-of-play area holding cards collected for their victory points during a scenario or its resolution. The victory points of its current contents are distinct from the total experience awarded by a scenario resolution.
_Avoid_: Encounter discard pile, earned experience

**Investigator discard pile**:
An investigator's ordered, face-up pile of discarded cards they own, including discarded weaknesses. Its contents are public information and out of play.
_Avoid_: Encounter discard pile, removed-from-game cards

**Skill icon**:
A card symbol associated with willpower, intellect, combat or agility, or a wild symbol that can match any of those tested skills. Applicable icons on committed cards contribute to the investigator's skill value for that test.
_Avoid_: Strength or power when referring to combat

**Wild icon**:
A skill icon that matches any of the four tested skills; under the ordinary commitment rules, each contributes +1 to the skill being tested. Its printed identity remains wild even when its contribution is expressed as a bonus to a particular skill.
_Avoid_: A fifth tested skill, permanent icon conversion

**Committable card**:
A card that can legally be added to the current skill test's pending commitment, given the cards already selected and all applicable rules.
_Avoid_: Matching-icon card when implying complete commitment eligibility

**Pending commitment**:
The set of cards selected for a skill test but not yet confirmed. Selection remains changeable while the commitment decision is open.
_Avoid_: Committed cards when referring to an unconfirmed selection

**Base skill value**:
The investigator's value for the tested skill before the test's modifiers are applied. Committed icons and applicable card and chaos-token effects contribute to the modified skill value, not to this base.
_Avoid_: Skill subtotal including commitments

**Chaos-token modifier**:
The contribution of resolved chaos tokens to a test's skill value. It is one kind of skill modifier; committed icons and applicable card abilities can also modify the skill value.
_Avoid_: Modifier when referring exclusively to chaos tokens

**Skill test result**:
The determined success or failure of a test, together with its final skill value, difficulty and applicable margin after modifiers and special rules are applied. It is distinct from a provisional outcome and from the consequences of that result.
_Avoid_: Token modifier, result consequences when referring only to the determined outcome

**Reveal group**:
Chaos-token reveals governed together by one instruction or replacement effect. A nested group can remain one candidate for its containing instruction while retaining several physical tokens.
_Avoid_: Outer reveal without naming its source, tokens added by Jacqueline when referring to her cancellation scope

**Held chaos token**:
A physical chaos token drawn from the bag and not yet returned to its rules-defined destination. Resolving or canceling its effects does not itself return it; a historical record of its face does not mean the token is still held.
_Avoid_: Resolved token when referring to its current location

**Investigator standee**:
The upright representation of an investigator beside the location they occupy, distinct from their full investigator card in their investigator area.
_Avoid_: Investigator card when referring to the figure on the map

**Location standee slot**:
A designated position beside a location card or named location marker for an investigator standee. It represents presence at that location, not a separate game location or a limit on legal occupancy.
_Avoid_: Space on the location card, investigator area

**Prepared position**:
An explicitly arranged game state from which a bounded play task begins. It does not imply that campaign setup or the preceding turns have been played through.
_Avoid_: Fresh campaign, scripted outcome

**Scenario attempt**:
One play of a scenario, with its own starting state, player decisions and random outcomes. Reopening continues the same attempt; restarting begins a new one.
_Avoid_: Replay when referring to a new attempt

**Scenario**:
A named adventure from Arkham Horror: The Card Game, such as The Gathering. A scenario is distinct from a particular attempt to play it.
_Avoid_: Experiment, mode

**Source archive**:
The externally owned Arkham Horror LCG resource collection from which material is selected for this application. It remains intact when selected assets are prepared for use.
_Avoid_: Installed assets, prepared asset collection

**Prepared asset collection**:
The self-contained selection of presentation assets supplied for Arkham Unfolded's supported content. It is separate from the source archive and from personal saves and settings.
_Avoid_: Source archive, cache
